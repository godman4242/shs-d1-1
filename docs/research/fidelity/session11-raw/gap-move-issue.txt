Zero stroke-free movement ticks exist in combat. Timing constants: stroke->movement windup 133-200 ms; dash 2-3 ticks; the stroke VANISHES ON THE FIRST DASH TICK (this corrects the previously-established "vanishes when the player lands"); post-landing the character is pixel-static for 733-1133 ms and the shortest landing->next-stroke interval is 533 ms, so a lockout <=533 ms cannot be falsified. [gap G] (NOT-FOUND/high) Q2 — can the character move WHILE a stroke is being drawn?
   VALUE: UNRESOLVABLE AT THE SAMPLING FLOOR. In all 4 strokes measured, the stroke goes from completely absent on one tick to FULL FINAL LENGTH on the next (66.7 ms).    NOTE : Either every drag in this footage was faster than 67 ms, or the game only renders the stroke on mouse-up. Cannot distinguish. Safe build choice: render the stroke live while dragging and start movement on release.

[gap G] (INFERRED/medium) Sampling / engine tick: the ORIGINAL GAME ran at ~24 fps, not 30
