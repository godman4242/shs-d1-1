### gap-enemy-ai (16 rows)
SUMMARY: Filled gap H from video A (video B's castle window yielded a clean NOT-FOUND for speed — the camera pans nonstop with no world anchor). 16 rows, every one backed by a PNG in /Users/kheshav/kheshav-code/SSHD 1-1/reference/frames/session11/gap-enemy-ai/ (30 frames, re-extracted at native 1920x1080 from the source and verified to reproduce the JPEG measurements exactly).

Headline results:
1. APPROACH SPEED — 8 enemies, camera staticity proven before every reading: 13.1-20.1 px/content-tick @1080 (197-302 px/s), median 16.0 px/tick = 240 px/s @1080 = 8.5 stage px/tick = 128 stage px/s. The previous pass's "~22 px/tick" is the PEAK step of a stuttering walk (deltas alternate ~11/~23 with occasional 0/34); the mean is 17-18. All 8 use the upright short-step WALK; the deep-lean bounding run appears ONLY as the attack lunge (60.5 px/tick @1080 = 32.3 stage px/tick, ~3.3x walk).
2. CADENCE — 10 hit-to-hit intervals from two 1v1 duels (JINBEI (4), BANDIT LEADER (6)): min 0.467 s (7 ticks), median 0.733 s, mean 0.853 s, max 1.933 s.
3. TELEGRAPH — pose only, no FX. 0.600 s (9 ticks) for an approach attack, measured twice independently — the build's >=520 ms assertion HOLDS for that case. But a point-blank repeat swing on a downed player is only 0.267 s (4 ticks), so a single global >=520 ms gate is wrong. Also: the red stroke line seen near attacking enemies belongs to the PLAYER, not the enemy — enemies get no drawn line.
4. RANGE — the enemy attack is a dash-THROUGH, not a radius check. Commit distance 364.5 px @1080 (194 stage px); the enemy runs through the player and damage resolves at the END of the dash, 2-3 ticks after the pass, with the enemy already 180 px @1080 (96 stage px) past on the far side. Same slash-dash mechanic as the player.
5. SIMULTANEITY — enemies take turns. With 8 bars on screen the player's hit rate matches a 1v1 (intervals 0.533/0.933/0.800/2.400 s); 4-6 enemies are pixel-static for >=1.5 s while 1 (max 2) engages. Implement an attack token.
6. BLOCKING — clean NOT-FOUND. Zero blocks, parries, dodges or flinches in ~70 s of combat; a sweeping stroke removes exactly the bars it crosses. Two positives instead: named enemies LEAP (bar rides 150 px @1080 above the ground row, HP unchanged so not knockback), and waves SPAWN by dropping in from above.
