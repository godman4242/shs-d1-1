GAP B — NAMED-ENEMY HP. The named-enemy HP bar is a FIXED 130 px track @1080 (69.3 stage px) with a left-anchored green fill and a dark-red remainder — this CORRECTS the prior ledger entry that said the widget's total width shrinks. JINBEI (4) has 132 HP (range 130-134) and dies in 3 basic slashes; the winning fight was exactly 42+48+42 = 132. HONDA SHIZUKA (7) has ~44 HP (range 43-45) and also died in 3 hits. Critical trap avoided: JINBEI's 1 px = 1 HP is a coincidence — the bar is a PERCENTAGE bar and px-per-HP differs per enemy (0.985 vs 2.95). Per-hit implied totals are reported individually so the scatter (122-138 for JINBEI) is visible; cumulative fits are the reliable numbers.

GAP C — DAMAGE IS A ROLL, NOT A FIXED NUMBER. Six basic slashes on ONE target with one stance (HEAVY), one weapon and one level (5) gave 36, 30, 27, 42, 48, 42 — min 27, max 48, mean 37.5, 1.78x spread, with 42 repeating. So core/damage.ts DOES need a randomised min/max fed by the injected RNG. A key disambiguation: the anomalous 10 popup coincides exactly with CHI dropping 20/20 -> 18/20 and a blue VFX, so it is a CHI-skill hit and must be excluded — CHI was untouched on all six basic hits and on both video-B hits. A second independent sample (level 10, different weapon/target) gave 21 and 17, same qualitative result. One honest caveat, tagged INFERRED/low: a stroke-length confound test shows two near-identical strokes (192 vs 190 px) dealing 42 vs 48, which argues weakly against length-scaled damage, but only the residual stroke is drawn so this is not settled.

Two NOT-FOUND items are reported cleanly rather than guessed: HONDA SHIZUKA's third/killing popup (covered by the death wipe) and, by extension, her exact overkill margin.
