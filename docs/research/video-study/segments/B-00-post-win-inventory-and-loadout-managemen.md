## Feel               (pacing, camera, animation weight, FX; if combat: rough per-encounter durations in seconds)

- Overall pacing is slow and menu-heavy: roughly 100 of the 150 seconds are spent in stacked UI panels (Ninja Services → reward popup → Quests → Skills → Player/equipment → Stats → World Map → Quests again) rather than in the game world, reflecting a deliberate "inventory management" tutorial-style segment.
- World-view camera is a static side-scroller framing, player sprite walks left/right at a modest, unhurried pace across painted stone-wall and later forest/mountain-vista backgrounds; parallax mountain layers visible in the outdoor scenic shots (t=00:52 onward) give a sense of depth.
- Combat encounters are extremely short and low-stakes: each of the two duels captured resolves in roughly **3–6 seconds** of active swordplay (based on 2-second frame sampling showing only 1–2 distinct hit-combo states before the win screen), consistent with fodder-tier "Bandit (6)" enemies rather than a boss fight.
- Death/win transition FX: a hard cut to solid dark red background with black silhouettes, held briefly, then a jagged red-brushstroke "YOU WIN" title stamps in — a consistent, punchy stinger used both times combat resolves in this segment.
- Combat-start FX: a distinct screen-wipe transition (background torn into 3 horizontal strips that slide) with a bold "FIGHT!" brush-text stamp, used once before the second duel (not shown before the first, which may have already been mid-fight at segment start).
- UI panels use a warm brown/parchment/gold aesthetic throughout (samurai-era scroll and lacquer motif), consistent across Ninja Services, Quests, Skills, Player, World Map, and popup modals — a cohesive "ink and parchment" visual language distinct from the painted combat backgrounds.
- Menu interactions are relatively snappy — no visible loading delays between panel opens; the SEARCH POT interaction is the only in-world action with a visible progress/loading bar (~1-2s).
