## Story/dialogue     (OWN-WORDS summary; names/quest titles OK; no verbatim dialogue)
- The player (guest samurai) treks from the eastern Toshibara Forest, through a cave, then south and southeast back through forest paths, into a stand of stone lanterns/graves (bamboo grove area), finally arriving at Toshibara Castle Town's gate and entrance district. This is a straightforward fetch-quest traversal: find and pick a specific herb (Black Bald Herb) somewhere in the forest/cave, then return it to Yoko in town.
- Yoko greets the player warmly on delivery, expresses that she'd been wanting this herb for a while, and rewards the player. Immediately afterward she pivots to a new interaction: she offers a barter — she'll trade a fancy pair of enchanted pants (fire/crit-themed) in exchange for a different pair of pants (health-themed) that the player doesn't currently carry. Rather than declining outright, the game offers to track this as an open trade quest so the player can complete it once they find the needed item.
- Overall narrative beat: introduces Yoko as a recurring quest-giver/vendor-adjacent NPC who both hands out herb-gathering fetch quests and runs a player-to-NPC item barter system (distinct from a straight coin-shop), broadening the side-content loop beyond pure combat/mining quests seen earlier.
