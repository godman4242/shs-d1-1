## Story/dialogue     (OWN-WORDS summary; names/quest titles OK; no verbatim dialogue)
Mai is upset and struggling to explain what happened. Satoshi is surprised and relieved to reunite with an old ally, Fumio (referred to as "old guard" comrades). Satoshi laments that a plan involving someone named Hiroshi has gone off track. Mai, still distressed, reveals that her father was ambushed by armed men. A branching dialogue-topic prompt lets the player pick between hearing "Mai's here?" or "What's Satoshi planning?" — both threads play out. It's established the group needs to rescue a man named Yasuka, and that the same bounty hunters who attacked the party on the road are responsible. Mai recounts that her father tried to resist but his old leg injury slowed him down; the attackers bound him and threatened to kill him unless he gave up the location of the "Old Guard" hideout. Satoshi identifies the bounty-hunter leader as "Tadao" and describes him with open contempt — calling him a cowardly, self-styled "big shot" who takes the dishonorable jobs (assassinations, poisonings) that more principled bounty hunters won't touch, and saying he's beneath even the label of vermin. Fumio defends Yasuka, insisting he'd never betray the Old Guard's location. Satoshi decides he and Fumio will stay behind to guard Mai, reasoning that since the bounty hunters (specifically Tadao) already know Fumio and Satoshi by sight as former Old Guard members, their presence near the search area would tip off the hideout's general location — so the player is sent alone to investigate the bounty hunters' whereabouts and report back. The scene establishes Yasuka (Mai's father) as the captive, Tadao as the named bounty-hunter leader/antagonist, and "Old Guard" as the group's shared former affiliation.
