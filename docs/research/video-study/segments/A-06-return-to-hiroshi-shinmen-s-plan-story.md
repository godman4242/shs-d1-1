## Story/dialogue     (OWN-WORDS summary; names/quest titles OK; no verbatim dialogue)
- The player treks back from Kasuta Mountain Path through Kasuta Field and Kasuta Forest to Hiroshi's hut, following the "Return to Hiroshi's Hut" quest marker.
- Inside the hut, Hiroshi greets the player warmly, saying he wants them to meet someone — introducing a young swordsman named Shinmen, who says Hiroshi has spoken well of the player.
- The player gets a dialogue choice for how to respond to the introduction; picking the "tell the truth" option leads to a joking exchange where the player admits Hiroshi described them unflatteringly (roughly "a bit slow/simple"), which amuses both Shinmen and Hiroshi rather than offending them.
- Once the introductions settle, the player is given a chance to ask Shinmen about himself or about "the Shogun" before moving on. Shinmen explains he has served a lord named Masahiro as chief retainer since the end of a past conflict referred to as the "Demon Wars," and separately notes that the Shogun cares deeply for the land and its people and wants to see them prosper.
- Hiroshi then asks Shinmen to lay out the plan for the player (matching the segment's title "Shinmen's plan"). Shinmen explains: a winemaker named Yasuka, a retired ("too lame to fight directly") veteran of the "Old Guard," runs an inn in a nearby town. Shinmen instructs the player to travel to Yasuka's inn and deliver a coded message — asking him to "prepare the toast to the Shogun's health" — which will cue Yasuka to prepare a wine shipment for Hiroshi's group to deliver to "the palace." This is clearly a smuggling/cover operation for the group's real (likely rebellion-related) plan.
- Shinmen also reports that he has delivered recruitment messages to members of the "Old Guard": two named allies, Ito and Takahasi, have agreed to help, while a third named Yuudai has declined, saying he's done with fighting.
- The quest updates to "Travel to the town and speak to Yasuka at the inn," sending the player back out through the forest, where they're intercepted mid-field by a "Ruffian" enemy and fight a short skirmish before continuing south toward a new area.
