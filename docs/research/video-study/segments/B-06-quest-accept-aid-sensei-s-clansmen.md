## Story/dialogue     (OWN-WORDS summary; names/quest titles OK; no verbatim dialogue)
Immediately before this segment's cutscene, the player finishes off a straggler and collects loot/XP, then treks from a mountain-path waypoint down toward Kakuchi Castle Town's gates. An "Episode 2" title card plays, followed by a short in-fiction poem about wind-battered swallows helping each other stay on course — thematically foreshadowing the "aid" quest to come.

At the town gates the player finds a winded, out-of-breath traveler (Fumio) who's been running from pursuers and jokes about always being "late for his own funeral." The player is offered a branching response (sympathetic concern vs. dismissal); choosing to engage, Fumio explains he belongs to a group called the Shira Tancho and promises a reward if the player helps him. The player accepts, framing it explicitly as aiding their sensei's clansmen (confirming the Shira Tancho are tied to the player's own teacher/faction), and then asks whether Fumio knows a fellow clan member named Satoshi. Before that thread resolves, pursuers catch up and a fight breaks out — the player and Fumio team up against the ambushers and win.

Afterward Fumio properly introduces himself and thanks the player. A follow-up branching conversation lets the player probe Fumio's standing in the Shira Tancho vanguard or whether he's heard about the shogun's death; Fumio claims to have been one of the group's more important (if not top-ranking) members. The player then delivers grim news of their own: a man named Masahiro has been murdered. Fumio reacts with surprise, saying it's lucky he ran into the player when he did — implying this news matters to the wider plot and to the Shira Tancho specifically.

The scene returns to the overworld with the story quest now pointing the player toward "the old guard hideout," and the player is stopped both by an over-capacity inventory (blocking travel) and by a town guard demanding a toll to enter Kakuchi Castle Town.
