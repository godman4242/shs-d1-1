## Mechanics observed (rules, numbers, timings, damage/HP/cost values you can read — cite frame t=MM:SS for each claim)

- **Search Pot costs 2 AP** per interaction, confirmed verbatim on-screen twice: t=1:00:51 ("S Chikada Mountain Path") and t=1:01:14 ("E Chikada Field"). Both are world-map pot props (a clay jar/urn sprite sitting on the ground) that trigger a proximity action prompt.
- **AP is NOT shown in the standard overworld HUD.** Across ~15 overworld/exploration frames in this window (t=59:34 through t=1:01:49), the bottom-left status cluster only ever shows HP and CHI bars plus stance label — never an AP bar or number. AP is only visible via the Player > Stats screen. This means the player cannot see their current AP total without opening the menu while exploring — a UX/recreation detail worth flagging.
- **Neither Search Pot prompt was actually triggered/consumed in this window** — in both cases (t=1:00:51 and t=1:01:14) the very next frame shows the player already having walked past/away from the pot with no "you received" loot modal specific to a pot, and no coin/AP change tied to that moment. The only reward popup captured in this segment (t=1:01:04, "YOU RECEIVED ×5 [scroll icon], XP 52") follows directly from winning the bandit duel (t=1:00:59 "YOU WIN"), not from a pot. So this window shows the AP **cost label** clearly, but not a confirmed before/after AP spend — flagged explicitly per instructions since the pot-search interaction itself wasn't completed on-camera.
- **Player stats panel AP reading, single earlier instance** (t=59:16–59:27, in the pre-segment Player menu, not tied to a pot): AP bar reads 27/40 at t=59:16, then 28/40 at t=59:27, with HP (12/30), CHI (47/47), and coins (782) all unchanged between those two frames. This is consistent with slow **passive AP regeneration** while the menu is open/idle (no player action occurred between the two reads) — one AP point gained over roughly 10 seconds of real time, though the exact regen cadence can't be pinned down from two samples. A small "7" numeral appears directly under the AP bar in these frames (possibly a per-tick regen amount or a countdown, unconfirmed from visuals alone).
- **HP regenerates over time/after combat**: HP was 12/30 through the mine/mountain traversal (t=59:16–1:01:04) and had risen to 14/30 by t=1:01:07, shortly after the bandit fight concluded — consistent with either passive regen or a small post-victory HP tick (no explicit heal item was used on-screen).

Separately, coins rose from 782 to 792 (+10) and XP from 27056 to 27108 (+52, matching the loot popup) between t=59:16 and t=1:01:07, i.e., the full +10 coin, +52 XP gain is attributable to this one bandit kill.

## Feel               (pacing, camera, animation weight, FX; if combat: rough per-encounter durations in seconds)

- Movement between zones is brisk — a run cycle covers a full screen-width in roughly 2–3 seconds, and zone transitions (e.g., mine → mountain path → field → forest) are near-instant with only a short black-frame flash/loading beat (~1 frame in this capture, i.e., under half a second at this sampling rate).
- The overworld art shifts noticeably by biome: the mine interior is a flat, low-saturation stone-tile background with static crates/barrels/vases as set dressing; the mountain path and fields use painterly parallax mountain backgrounds with drifting cloud-streak FX; the forest is a darker teal-grey palette with tall bare tree silhouettes, giving a noticeably more ominous read right before the bandit ambush.
- Combat is fast and punchy: the observed duel is a straightforward 3-hit-combo exchange with a visible red hit-flash and a small debris/shard particle burst on a landed hit (t=1:00:43), plus a stray arrow projectile from the bandit at t=1:00:46 (bandit appears to be an archer-type here despite closing to melee range). Total encounter length from first contact to the "YOU WIN" screen is roughly 20–24 seconds.
- The "YOU WIN" screen and loot modal use a hard cut to a saturated red vignette with rough brush-stroke typography — a deliberately more aggressive, punch-in stinger compared to the otherwise muted exploration palette.
- Pot props are small, static, single-frame sprites (no idle animation observed) planted in the grass near sign-posts; the "Search Pot: 2 AP" prompt appears to be a simple proximity trigger box rather than requiring a directional facing or a button press shown on-screen in this footage.
