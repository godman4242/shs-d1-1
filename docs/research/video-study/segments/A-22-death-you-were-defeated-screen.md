## Story/dialogue     (OWN-WORDS summary; names/quest titles OK; no verbatim dialogue)

The story quest tasks the player with tracking down and defeating a bandit boss named Inokichi inside the "N Chikada Dungeon." After clearing a lesser bandit mook in a quick one-hit-combo skirmish (rewarding 96 XP), the player advances east and encounters Inokichi directly. Inokichi taunts the player, mocking them as just another bounty hunter after his head and refusing to take the threat seriously. The player's guest character retorts that criminals must answer for their crimes and notes Inokichi carries a valuable bounty. A brief "pop, glug glug glug" sound cue plays — reads as Inokichi drinking something (implied liquid courage / buff item) right before the fight kicks off, which may explain why he's accompanied by extra allied bandits in the ensuing brawl. The fight that follows pits the player against Inokichi plus multiple other named/unnamed bandits simultaneously (a mini-boss gang-up rather than a 1v1 duel), and the player is overwhelmed and killed. Selecting the (implied, off-screen) "Free Respawn" option drops the player straight back into the same pre-fight exchange with Inokichi's gang, ready to try again.
