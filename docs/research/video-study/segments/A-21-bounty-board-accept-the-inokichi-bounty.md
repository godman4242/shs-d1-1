## Story/dialogue     (OWN-WORDS summary; names/quest titles OK; no verbatim dialogue)

The player exits West Chikada Forest, checks a territory-info popup confirming Chikada City is a protected/diplomatic zone that can't be conquered, then proceeds through the City Gates into the City Entrance and onward to Main Street. There they briefly consider talking to an NPC named Gojo Otondo or entering the inn, but continue past into a side district. They pass through what's labeled the Entertainment District, noting a gambling den entrance, before heading back toward Main Street. There, an NPC named Masatora is standing near a public bounty board flagged with an exclamation-point quest marker. The player opens the board, which offers a bounty: track down and defeat a bandit/rogue named Inokichi, bringing back his club as proof of the kill. The player accepts. This immediately grants an XP reward large enough to level the character up. With the fresh stat and skill points, the player opens the character sheet to raise ATK, DEF, and SPD, and opens the skills menu to learn a new "Focus" ability (a move that instantly refills the character's Focus/AP-related resource bar). The quest log confirms the objective is now "Locate and defeat Inokichi," pointing toward a dungeon area north of Chikada, reachable via a paid fast-travel option. The segment ends with the player leaving the city gates and heading back out into West Chikada Forest, presumably beginning the journey to that dungeon.
