## Story/dialogue     (OWN-WORDS summary; names/quest titles OK; no verbatim dialogue)
The game congratulates the player for reaching level 10 and unlocks the War Room, a clan-based end-game strategy layer, explaining that fighting for a clan yields coins, gear, and gold. The player browses short lore blurbs for candidate clans before joining. Clan Aka Ryu is framed as led by a foresighted lord who anticipated an approaching war upon learning the shogun had fallen ill, and who now controls the north-eastern domain. Clan Ki Sensu is described as a wealthy, non-confrontational clan under a lady leader forced to arm itself to protect its holdings. Clan Aoi Hoiru is led by a shrewd, well-connected lord who controls the south-western domain and leverages his information advantage. A "Random" option lets the game auto-assign a clan. After picking a clan the game plays a seven-page rules tutorial covering how to join ongoing battles via the map, how personal combat against enemy troops and rival players works, and how clan members can jointly fund and build structures (command posts, forward bases, barricades, tunnels, and defensible towns) to support the war effort. Joining a clan also triggers an achievement toast. Separately, the player is mid-way through an unrelated side quest to track down and defeat a named bandit/rival figure inside a mine.

## Recreation notes   (config values to add/adjust, which build Stage this maps to, anything contradicting the repo's spec/research docs)
- War Room is a substantial late-game clan-strategy meta-layer (clan selection, territory map, resource "crops", move budgets, structure building) layered on top of the core duel game — this is almost certainly out of scope for an early Stage of the 1:1 recreation and should be tracked as a distinct, much-later milestone (its own multi-stage system, not a single config addition).
- If/when planned, config values to capture: structure-build contribution cost 500 coins/player; Recruit rank move allowance 3/turn with an outer cap around 36 (unconfirmed whether per-day or per-cycle — needs a later segment to confirm reset cadence); clan resource "Crops" with a small HQ-based regen (+2 observed, base unclear).
- Player weight-capacity system confirmed here: cap 100, with over-cap states allowed (114/100 seen, red-highlighted) and a DROP ITEM confirmation flow — relevant to the core inventory/equipment system regardless of War Room scope.
- Equipment stat display format confirmed: current value plus "(EQ: n)" showing the equipped item's base contribution, across DMG (range), HP REGEN, WATER ATK, EARTH ATK, WEIGHT, DEFENSE, EARTH DEF, CRITICAL DAMAGE, MAX CHI, CHI REGEN — useful reference for the equipment/stats UI contract regardless of War Room timing.
- Text typos present in the source UI ("CAN MAY", "CONTRUCTION") are noted for authenticity tracking only — per project invariants we should NOT reproduce verbatim UI strings; use paraphrased equivalents for any in-repo copy.
- Clan Ki Sensu's card stating it also rules "the domain in the north-east" (same phrase as Aka Ryu, whereas Aoi Hoiru is explicitly south-west) looks like a likely copy-paste bug in the source game — flag as a known original-game inconsistency, not something to intentionally replicate.
