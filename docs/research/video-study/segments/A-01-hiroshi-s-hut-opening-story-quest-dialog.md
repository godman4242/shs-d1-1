## Story/dialogue     (OWN-WORDS summary; names/quest titles OK; no verbatim dialogue)

Guest (the player character) travels to Hiroshi's hut in W Kasuta Forest per a prologue quest from their unnamed sensei. Outside, a rough-looking hut resident (not Hiroshi himself, a minor NPC with a sickle-like tool) waves the player toward the entrance. Once close, Hiroshi — a large, bare-chested, confident swordsman carrying a naginata/glaive — steps out to greet the player warmly, apologizing for a "less-than-warm welcome" implying some earlier minor scuffle happened off-screen (the player brushes it off as "nothing I couldn't handle"). They head inside.

Indoors, Hiroshi explains that he and the player's sensei served together for years under a "Shogun Masahiro," and that he requested the player be sent to him because the Shogun himself needs help. The player is initially thrilled/cocky about being singled out by the Shogun, then more humbly asks what use they could possibly be. Hiroshi reassures them, citing the sensei's high praise, and says the task he has in mind is simple.

The player can branch into optional questions. One thread reveals a person or faction called "Shinmen" — described as a former member of a disbanded elite unit called "the Vanguard" (disbanded by Masahiro after "the Demon Wars" ended), who — unlike most Vanguard members who went their own way — stayed loyal to/close with Masahiro. This is framed as a possible internal threat to the Shogun ("who would want to harm the Shogun?" → Shinmen is a suspect option). Hiroshi assigns the main task: find and presumably question/deal with three men — **Ito, Takahasi, and Yuudai** — whose locations he'll mark on the player's map, while Hiroshi himself stays behind to await someone named "Shinmen" arriving at the hut. The player accepts eagerly ("I won't let you down"), and Hiroshi outfits them with a sword and supplies before they part ways.
