## Story/dialogue     (OWN-WORDS summary; names/quest titles OK; no verbatim dialogue)
- Player arrives at the Toshibara Castle Town Gates, greeted by an on-screen story-quest prompt telling them to travel into town and speak with Yasuka at the inn.
- Walking through the gate into the Castle Town Entrance, the player bumps into an elderly traveling merchant named Kojuro, who apologizes for not noticing them, then introduces himself. A dialogue-choice menu lets the player ask about his stall, ask for directions to the inn, or just leave — the player selects asking for the inn. Kojuro warns that traveling alone is dangerous and gives the player an item as a parting gift.
- The player continues into town and encounters a young woman NPC named Yoko standing near the inn entrance. She greets the player warmly ("hello, samurai") and opens a dialogue menu with Quest / Trade / Leave options. Choosing Quest, she offers a side-quest to fetch a "Black Bald Herb," which she says can be found east at Toshibara Field. The player accepts tentatively ("I'll see what I can do"), picking up the side quest.
- The player enters the inn. Inside, another NPC greets a patron and delivers a request on behalf of a "friend" — asking the seated patron (apparently named Masahiro, referenced but not seen speaking directly) to prepare a toast in the shogun's honor, noting he's thirsty.
- A young woman named Mai appears (addressed as "father" by someone) and briefly acknowledges a request with a polite affirmative, agreeing to something ("as you wish").
- Yasuka, a scarred, one-legged former samurai, is introduced. He explains he lost part of his leg to an undead ("walking corpse") enemy during something called the "Demon Wars," which ended his samurai career — establishing an in-world backstory of a past demon conflict and undead threats. He jokes about wanting to marry off a daughter (implied to be Mai) to a suitable young man.
- The player is given a dialogue-choice: ask about Yasuka's shogun service, take a "Town Tutorial" option to ask about how the town works, or end the conversation. The player picks the tutorial option.
- Yasuka then delivers the segment's key worldbuilding speech (paraphrased): a region's clan control is influenced heavily by how active local samurai are in completing quests for townsfolk — good rulers act, they don't just talk. He notes there are many opportunities in town for an enterprising ronin willing to look for them. He closes with the thematic point that a prosperous trade town can collapse into a bandit-infested ruin if neglected, while a humble village can rise to become an economic hub for the whole territory if a few vigilant samurai stand guard over it — directly tying player quest activity to a territory-control/reputation system.
- Completing this tutorial dialogue awards the player 250 XP and 5 of an item, then the town's inn menu (rest/buy/leave) becomes available with the innkeeper greeting the player and offering to restore Health, Chi, and AP for 8 coins.
