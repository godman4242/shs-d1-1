## Story/dialogue     (OWN-WORDS summary; names/quest titles OK; no verbatim dialogue)
- The player encounters a traveling NPC named Askikaga Masayori in the E Chikada Field, who has a quest marker over his head. A brief exchange establishes that the player offers help, and Masayori accepts, initiating an escort mission to guide him safely to the S Chikada Mountain Path.
- The escort quest doesn't show Masayori visibly walking alongside the player through most of the traversed rooms; he re-surfaces as a quest-trigger prompt near S Chikada Mine, implying he "catches up" or the escort is tracked abstractly rather than as a persistent on-screen companion sprite.
- Along the route, the player is ambushed twice: once by a bandit group in S Chikada Forest, and again — after entering S Chikada Mine — by a tougher bandit squad inside the mine's cave interior, which reaches level 10 for the player upon defeat.
- Reaching level 10 triggers a narrative/systemic beat: the game congratulates the player and opens access to the "War Room," where the player can pledge allegiance to one of several rival clans. The one detailed clan, Aka Ryu, is introduced as an ambitious, war-ready faction under a lord named Tsuyoshi, based in the northeastern part of the game's territory, having begun preparing for conflict after hearing the ruling Shogun had fallen ill.
- A separate, pre-existing bounty quest ("Defeat Hattori Kazushi," expiring same-day) is visible throughout via the Quests menu, offering a distinctly named legendary-sounding weapon reward — this is a parallel, unrelated side quest thread running concurrently with the escort mission.

## Feel               (pacing, camera, animation weight, FX; if combat: rough per-encounter durations in seconds)
- Non-combat traversal is brisk: the player sprints continuously left-to-right/right-to-left across rooms with a persistent running animation and cape/pack physics jiggle; room transitions are instant cuts (no visible loading screen) except for two black transition flash-frames caught mid-scene-swap (t=53:46, t=54:06).
- Combat framing shrinks the viewport into a letterboxed band (black bars top/bottom) for both fights, focusing attention on a tighter horizontal combat lane distinct from the full-height overworld view — this is a consistent "combat mode" visual treatment.
- Hit feedback is heavy: white/grey slash-impact particles, red blood splatter bursts, and ragdoll-style enemy stagger/knockback on hits; screen flashes solid red at two dramatic beats — the moment right before a "YOU WIN" resolves, and again as a low-HP warning cue during the cave fight (t=54:26).
- First (forest) encounter: "FIGHT!" banner to "YOU WIN" ≈15 seconds (t=53:19→53:34), feels like a quick skirmish against a handful of bandits.
- Second (cave/mine) encounter: observed combo/HP frames span roughly t=54:11 (enter mine, Bandit(6) approaches) to t=54:29 (win) — visible engaged-combat window ≈15–18 seconds, but escalates faster and hits harder, with a mid-fight enemy reinforcement wave and a near-death HP swing (6→2 out of 30), giving it a noticeably higher tension/pacing spike than the forest fight despite similar raw duration.
- The level-up → War Room unlock chains directly off the cave fight's win screen with no extra navigation needed, giving the whole escort-quest segment a "quest climax" pacing shape: travel → skirmish → travel → bigger skirmish → level milestone → new game-system reveal.
