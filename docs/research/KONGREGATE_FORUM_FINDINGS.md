# PART 1 — Combat, skills & enemies (SYNTHESIZED)

### combat-core

**Facts with exact numbers**

- Enemies deal a minimum "piercing" damage per strike that Defense cannot reduce, scaling with enemy level — measured floors: L1/L10/L12 = 1, L35 = 8, L36–37 = 9; a second poster estimates the formula as ~enemy level ÷ 4 ("piercing is estimated at (mob lvl/4)"). [How to grind for Levels and EXP, the easy way.](https://web.archive.org/web/20200719193759/https://www.kongregate.com/forums/12-kongregate-multiplayer-games/topics/493522-straw-hat-samurai-duels-how-to-grind-for-levels-and-exp-the-easy-way)
- The ÷4 rule also appears player-side: you always deal at least (your level ÷ 4) damage to enemies — with Red Oni the stated exception. [Equipments and how do they work exactly?](https://web.archive.org/web/20200719120130/https://www.kongregate.com/forums/12-kongregate-multiplayer-games/topics/486247-straw-hat-samurai-duels-equipments-and-how-do-they-work-exactly)
- Measured Defense breakpoints: at level 60, ~150 Defense forces same-level mobs down to that player's floor of 14 damage per hit; ~200–250 Defense recommended by level 80 for grinding. [Can someone explain how Attack and Defense, and Fire Attack and Fire Defense work?](https://web.archive.org/web/20200719191051/https://www.kongregate.com/forums/12-kongregate-multiplayer-games/topics/486679-straw-hat-samurai-duels-can-someone-explain-how-attack-and-defense-and-fire-attack-and-fire-defense-work)
- 1 point of Attack adds ≈ +1 minimum and +1 maximum damage — player measurement, "in balance stance at least"; Piercing and Critical raise your min and max slash damage. ### skills-meters

**Facts with exact numbers**
